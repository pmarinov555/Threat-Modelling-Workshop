**Controls Required for Insider Attack Targeting Quant Algorithms:**

- **Privileged Access Management (PAM):** Restrict and monitor access to quant algorithm repositories.
- **Behavioral Analytics:** Detect anomalous data access/transfer patterns by insiders.
- **Data Loss Prevention (DLP):** Block unauthorized exfiltration of algorithm code.
- **Code Integrity Checks:** Ensure algorithms are not modified without approval.
- **Segregation of Duties:** Separate roles for algorithm development, testing, and deployment.
- **Audit Logs:** Track all access and modifications to algorithm repositories.
- **Encryption-at-Rest:** Protect stored quant algorithms from unauthorized extraction.
